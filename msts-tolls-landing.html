The problem

Europe's toll map was built to be complicated.

Fragmentation

A different contract in every country

Over 140 toll chargers and government agencies, each with its own registration, deposit, top-up rules and support line. Cross three borders and you're managing three relationships before lunch.

Incompatible tech

GNSS and DSRC don't talk

Some countries charge by satellite-tracked distance, others by microwave gantry. Country-specific boxes pile up on the windscreen — and one wrong unit means a missed charge and a fine.

Admin drag

Reconciliation eats your week

Statements arrive in different formats, languages and cycles. Matching toll spend back to vehicles and jobs is manual, slow, and where margin quietly leaks away.

Compliance risk

Fines you can't read

Penalty notices land in languages your team doesn't speak, on deadlines you didn't see. Missed CO₂ class evidence puts every truck in the most expensive band by default.
